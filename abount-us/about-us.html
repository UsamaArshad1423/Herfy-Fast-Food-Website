ABOUT US

MADE FOR YOU. MADE THE HERFY WAY.

Since 1981, Herfy has been dedicated to serving tasty, high-quality meals with fast, friendly service and unbeatable value. What started as a single restaurant in Saudi Arabia has grown into the Kingdom's favorite fast food brand.

Taste it. Love it.

OUR STORY

Herfy was founded in 1981 in Jeddah with a simple vision: to create delicious food that brings people together. From our first restaurant, we focused on quality, hygiene, and great taste.

Today, with hundreds of branches across the Kingdom and beyond, we continue to stay true to our promise of serving food the Herfy way—made with care, served with a smile.

OUR MISSION

To serve delicious, high-quality meals with fast, friendly service while creating memorable experiences for our customers every day.

OUR VISION

To be the leading fast food brand in the region, known for quality, innovation, and commitment to customers and communities.
